Collaboration is not ordinary tool use

A tool does not become a collaborator merely by formatting a document, correcting spelling, storing files or executing an instruction. Contributor credit records participation that materially shaped ideas, methods, criticism, structure or decisions and was deliberately reviewed or adopted.

Sources are also not collaborators merely because they inform the work. Episodes, manuals, websites, archive captures and research materials should be cited according to their evidential role and rights status.
