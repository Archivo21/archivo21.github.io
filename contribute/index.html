Contribute

Useful work begins with honest scope

Questions, corrections, source suggestions, experiments and carefully scoped contributions are welcome. Formal roles are assigned through the project’s documented recruitment and review process.

Immediate priority

Video Editor Specialist

The project holds test and working recordings that require careful, non-destructive editing: shorter public demonstrations, privacy-aware audio treatment, captions and a transparent record of meaningful edits. Review copies can be shared directly with prospective editors; recording links are not listed publicly here.

  • Preserve evidential context over dramatic pacing.
  • Keep untouched originals separate from edited demonstrations.
  • Document cuts, cleaning, captions and transformations.
  • State uncertainty rather than repairing the story after the fact.

Other profiles

Many forms of expertise

ANHQV continuity, Sims 2 building or modding, archives, rights, transcription, subtitles, translation, architecture, GitHub, documentation and independent review.

Before contributing

Distinguish evidence from memory or interpretation, cite sources precisely, respect privacy and rights, document uncertainty and expect review. One useful correction does not require pretending to know the entire project.
