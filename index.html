Current project

ANHQV × The Sims 2 Preservation Project

v0.1 Research Preview · early research and pre-alpha reconstruction

Aquí Sims Hay Quien Viva

The project preserves, studies, reconstructs and makes inhabitable the world of Aquí no hay quien viva through The Sims 2.

ANHQV is the cultural subject and emotional centre. The game is the medium, laboratory and simulation system used to rebuild and investigate that world.
