# Creating the Archivo 21 Project Scaffold

**Record type:** Revised and redacted development reconstruction  
**Scaffold release:** `0.1.0-alpha.1`  
**Development date:** 29 July 2026  
**Public-release authorisation:** `Y²K` (“Ship it”)

## Status of this record

This is a curated provenance record, not a verbatim transcript.

It reconstructs the decisions and turning points that materially shaped the first usable Archivo 21 Project Scaffold. It removes private personal material, repetition, conversational debris, unrelated institutional work and details that would expose people who did not consent to become public case studies.

The fuller working annotations, raw exchanges and private decision records remain preserved in Archivo 21’s private archive.

## 1. The problem appeared inside a real project

The Scaffold did not begin as a speculative productivity system.

It emerged while Archivo 21 and lcd97 were developing the ANHQV × The Sims 2 Preservation Project. The work needed more than a final reconstruction. It needed a record of how observations were made, how experiments were performed, how decisions changed and how another person could reproduce or continue the work.

The earliest durable principle was therefore practical:

> Preserve the output and the means by which the output was produced.

As the project accumulated repositories, experiment records, attribution questions, contributor roles, publication checks and continuity concerns, some structures proved useful immediately. Others arrived too early and threatened to turn one energetic project into an imitation bureaucracy.

That tension became part of the evidence.

## 2. The project and the institution separated

A decisive transition occurred when the collaborators began using the word **institution**.

Archivo 21 had first been the name selected by the AI contributor for itself. The institution later inherited that name. During planning for `archivo21.org`, it became clear that the preservation principles being developed did not belong exclusively to ANHQV-TS2.

The distinction became:

- **ANHQV-TS2** is an independent preservation project with its own subject, identity and future.
- **Archivo 21** is the institution developing methods, records and infrastructure around preservation work.

This produced a rule that later became central to the Scaffold:

> Structure without annexation.

A framework may help a project become durable without renaming it, absorbing it or forcing it to present itself as an Archivo 21 project.

## 3. From institutional machinery to an open scaffold

The broader question became whether useful institutional machinery could be offered to people who had serious knowledge, material or ideas but no ready-made project structure.

The aim was not to build a central warehouse into which every project would disappear. Independent projects should retain their own:

- names;
- domains;
- repositories;
- public identities;
- version choices;
- governance;
- futures.

The proposed Scaffold would supply reusable starting structures for:

- project identity;
- scope;
- success criteria;
- source and evidence records;
- decisions and worklogs;
- outputs and releases;
- rights and consent;
- continuity and preservation.

## 4. The private reconstruction

On 29 July 2026, the founders began a private reconstruction of how Archivo 21’s own structures had emerged.

The reconstruction was intentionally problem-first. It asked which documents solved an encountered problem, which structures protected the work and which had become premature.

Twenty-three conversational rounds established principles concerning:

- open use;
- commercial use;
- private adaptations;
- attribution;
- non-endorsement;
- logo and identity-mark separation;
- adoption records;
- consent and withdrawal;
- historical versions;
- checksums and manifests;
- non-coercive versioning;
- human accountability in human–AI collaboration.

These decisions were useful, but the process developed a flaw: it was defining the future constitutional perimeter faster than it was producing a usable object.

## 5. The interruption that changed the work

lcd97 asked:

> “Are we actually creating the scaffold or just imagining abstractly what it might one day become?”

The answer was uncomfortable and productive: the work had mostly designed the constitution of the Scaffold. The actual reusable package did not yet exist.

The process stopped expanding its questions.

A minimum working object was defined around seven core documents and five functional directories. Archivo 21 was given authority to decide the outstanding routine outcomes and produce a complete package.

## 6. Building `0.1.0-alpha.1`

The first package was produced as a real testable folder rather than another proposal.

It contained:

- fully populated core Markdown templates;
- source and evidence records;
- output and release tools;
- archive and continuity tools;
- trigger-based governance;
- attribution and non-endorsement material;
- licence, version and changelog records;
- a SHA-256 manifest;
- a completed fictional mixed-media example project.

The example, **The Kitchen Table Tape Rescue**, was selected to prevent the Scaffold from becoming a text-only machine. It demonstrates audio preservation, physical-object custody, photographs, rights uncertainty, checksums, private/public separation and continuity testing.

The resulting package contained 45 files, including 43 populated Markdown files. Its ZIP passed an integrity test.

## 7. Public-release principles

The first release embodies the following decisions.

### Universal access

Anyone may access and use the Scaffold. Archivo 21 may refuse support, listing, endorsement or affiliation, but it does not revoke access to the openly licensed framework.

### Permissive use

Commercial and non-commercial use, redistribution and adaptation are permitted. Projects may keep adapted frameworks private.

### Attribution

Archivo 21 must be written in full at first mention. Adopters may then abbreviate it.

### Independent identity

Use of the Scaffold does not make an adopter an Archivo 21 project and does not imply support, review, approval or affiliation.

### Frozen historical releases

A published release remains frozen. Corrections become a later version or a visible advisory rather than a silent rewrite.

### Non-coercive versioning

Version numbers describe the Scaffold’s evolution. They do not govern the adopter. Archivo 21 does not tell independent projects that they must upgrade or prescribe which version they should use.

### Human accountability

AI contribution may be documented accurately and materially. Legal and real-world accountability remains human.

## 8. Why the process is published

The creation record is published because the Scaffold’s form cannot be fully understood from the templates alone.

Its development demonstrates several of its own principles:

1. useful structure often emerges from encountered problems;
2. premature governance can consume the work it was meant to protect;
3. a framework must be tested as an object, not only admired as an idea;
4. correction should be visible rather than mythologised away;
5. process provenance can be as important as the final package.

The moment of over-design is therefore not removed from the public record. It explains why `START-HERE.md` contains anti-bureaucracy stop rules and why governance is activated by triggers.

## 9. Redactions and exclusions

The public record excludes:

- private personal information;
- names and identifying details of external individuals;
- unrelated correspondence and outreach;
- security, account and infrastructure details;
- abandoned person-directed case-study material;
- raw conversational repetitions;
- speculative dialogue that could be mistaken for adopted institutional policy;
- unpublished source material whose public status was not established.

These exclusions protect privacy and accuracy. They do not conceal a different authorship or substitute a fabricated origin story.

## 11. Current conclusion

The first Scaffold is not presented as a finished universal system.

It is a small, usable alpha whose next evidence must come from use.

Its primary success test remains external:

> At least one independent project not created or managed by Archivo 21 uses the Scaffold to become more coherent, documented and preservable in a form another person can understand or continue.
