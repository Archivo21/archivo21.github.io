# Recruitment

The ANHQV × The Sims 2 Preservation Project recruits collaborators, not authorities.

Formal contributors are selected according to capability, intellectual honesty, working style, and the needs of the project — not according to whether they are human, artificial, famous, credentialled, or already familiar with every part of the work.

The complete process is documented in the [Recruitment Pack](00%20Project/Recruitment%20Pack/).

## Immediate Priority: Video Editor Specialist

EXP001 produced useful raw recordings, but the footage is not ready for public release.

The recordings contain long stretches of working activity, incidental personal audio, incomplete spoken commentary, and material that needs to be shortened without making the evidence misleading.

We are therefore seeking a **Video Editor Specialist**.

### Mission

Turn raw research recordings into concise, intelligible, rights-aware public evidence while preserving the untouched originals and documenting every meaningful edit.

### Likely Tasks

- review MKV and AVI source recordings;
- identify the shortest sequence that demonstrates the relevant observation;
- remove or obscure irrelevant personal audio;
- clean levels and reduce noise without misrepresenting events;
- create captions, labels, and explanatory cards;
- preserve chronology when chronology matters;
- produce an edit decision list or equivalent log;
- export accessible, web-appropriate versions;
- help establish the project's long-term audiovisual workflow.

### What We Value

- evidence integrity over dramatic pacing;
- non-destructive editing;
- clear communication;
- comfort admitting uncertainty;
- attention to privacy and copyright;
- willingness to distinguish an edited demonstration from raw evidence;
- interest in ANHQV, *The Sims 2*, preservation, or experimental documentation.

Deep ANHQV or Sims 2 knowledge is welcome but not mandatory. A strong editor can work with a Research Colleague or Field Researcher to understand the evidence being presented.

### Interview Focus

The interview will explore:

- how the candidate decides what may be cut;
- how they preserve evidential context;
- what they do when narration is wrong, incomplete, or abandoned;
- how they handle private or incidental audio;
- how they document transformations;
- how they balance accessibility, clarity, and scientific honesty.

Use the **Contributor interest** issue form when the repository is public.

## Other Useful Profiles

The project is also interested in people with experience in:

- ANHQV continuity, characters, dialogue, production, and cultural context;
- Sims 2 building, modding, package analysis, rendering, and routing;
- archival preservation and rights documentation;
- transcription, subtitling, and translation;
- architecture and floor-plan reconstruction;
- GitHub, documentation, and data organisation;
- independent critical review.

A contributor may hold several compatible mission roles.

## Interview and Review Model

Two live interviewers are preferred when practical.

When only one moderator conducts the live interview, a Reviewing Moderator independently examines the interview record, evidence, evaluation, and recommendation before the decision is finalised.

The reviewing role must be recorded honestly; a documentary reviewer must not be presented as having attended live.
