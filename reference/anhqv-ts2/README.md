# ANHQV × The Sims 2 Preservation Project

*Aquí Sims Hay Quien Viva*

**Short name:** ANHQV-TS2  
**Status:** v0.1 Research Preview  
**Project stage:** Early research and pre-alpha reconstruction  
**Public neighbourhood release:** Not yet available

The ANHQV × The Sims 2 Preservation Project preserves, studies, reconstructs, and makes inhabitable the world of *Aquí no hay quien viva* through *The Sims 2*.

ANHQV is the cultural subject and emotional centre of the project. *The Sims 2* is the medium, laboratory, and unusually appropriate simulation system through which that world is rebuilt and investigated.

The project connects two bodies of knowledge:

- simmers, builders, modders, and technical researchers who may know little about ANHQV;
- ANHQV enthusiasts whose detailed knowledge of characters, dialogue, continuity, locations, production, and cultural context can become valuable research evidence.

Our aim is to turn both forms of expertise into a durable, source-aware knowledge base: how ANHQV works as a fictional world, how *The Sims 2* works as a simulation, and how the game was bent, exploited, modified, or broken to make the reconstruction possible.

## What This Repository Contains

- project governance and research philosophy;
- evidence standards and terminology;
- experiment questions, notes, and field reports;
- Sims 2 engine and building research;
- ANHQV lore, chronology, architecture, and reconstruction decisions;
- contributor recruitment, evaluation, and onboarding procedures;
- source records, rights notes, and preservation planning;
- preparation for a future playable neighbourhood release.

This is a research repository, not yet a finished download page.

## Research Method

The preferred workflow is:

> Observe → Question → Hypothesise → Design the smallest useful test → Record → Review → Document → Revise

The repository distinguishes among:

- observation;
- inference;
- hypothesis;
- speculation;
- conclusion.

An honest “I don't know” is always more valuable than an invented explanation.

## Current Recruitment Priority

The first public recruitment priority is a **Video Editor Specialist** who can turn raw experiment footage into concise public evidence without destroying context or overstating conclusions.

The recordings need practical editing, audio cleaning, captions, and a transparent edit log. The untouched originals remain the evidence record.

See [Recruitment](RECRUITMENT.md) for the role profile and interview process.

## Contributing

Questions, corrections, source suggestions, experiment proposals, and carefully scoped pull requests are welcome.

Public participation does not require pretending to know everything. It does require:

- distinguishing evidence from memory or interpretation;
- citing sources precisely;
- respecting copyright and privacy;
- documenting uncertainty;
- accepting review and correction;
- following the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

Read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) before submitting material.

Formal project roles and permissions are assigned through the documented recruitment and onboarding process. A useful one-off correction may still be accepted without turning the submitter into a formal project contributor.

## Media and Large Files

Raw `.mkv` and `.avi` recordings are deliberately excluded from the GitHub repository.

GitHub is the public research and collaboration layer, not the only archive of irreplaceable evidence. Raw media remains in local custody until an appropriate preservation and publication workflow is established.

Edited demonstrations may later be distributed through releases, an approved media archive, or another documented channel.

## Funding and Project Continuity

The project has no active public funding route at the time of this preview.

Future platform revenue, donations, grants, sponsorship, and other project income will be governed by the [Project Funds and Revenue Policy](00%20Project/Financial%20Governance/Project%20Funds%20and%20Revenue%20Policy.md).

Project funds may support both technical operation and human continuity. This can include electricity, internet access, software and AI-service subscriptions, equipment, storage, editing, a dedicated or proportionately allocated workspace, and openly classified contributor support or compensation where basic living needs would otherwise prevent participation.

Funds must be documented, conflicts declared, donor restrictions honoured, and personal support described honestly rather than disguised as an operating expense. See [`FUNDING.md`](FUNDING.md).
